The Open Government Design System (OGDS) is a project by the People's Design System (PDS) community. A group of over 200 civic technologists have come together to make sure this critical work is resilient, stable, and trusted.

This is both a community AND a set of projects that we work on.

## Join our community

The People's Design System community offers several ways to collaborate together:

- Slack workspace
- Google Group mailing list
- Community video calls

## Use the Open Government Design System

The Open Government Design System (OGDS) is a set of projects:

- [OGDS Elements](https://github.com/open-government-design-system/ogds-elements/) provides custom elements , like Task List
- [OGDS Tokens](https://github.com/open-government-design-system/ogds-tokens) provides css variables of USWDS design tokens
- [OGDS Email Templates](https://github.com/open-government-design-system/ogds-email-templates) provides USWDS-styled email templates

## Contribute to our projects

### Filing a bug report

If you discovered a bug that no one else has reported in our [issues backlog (OGDS Elements)](https://github.com/open-government-design-system/ogds-elements/issues), let us know by [submitting an issue (OGDS Elements)](https://github.com/open-government-design-system/ogds-elements/issues/new).

### Starting a discussion

To share ideas, questions, or concerns with the OGDS core team and community, you can join any of our [open discussions (OGDS Elements)](https://github.com/open-government-design-system/ogds-elements/discussions) or [start a new one (OGDS Elements)](https://github.com/open-government-design-system/ogds-elements/discussions/new/choose).
